IMPORTANT DATA MODEL

The platform revolves around two connected records:

1. Asset
- A business system, application, database, SaaS service, vendor, or technical asset.
- Includes name, owner, business purpose, environment, data location, processors/third parties, and optional attachment.

2. Assessment
- Always linked to an existing Asset.
- Is the primary place to upload and assess files:
  TXT, CSV, XLSX, DOCX, PDF.
- After an assessment, the platform automatically produces:
  - detected data categories
  - classification
  - calculated risks
  - GDPR and ISO control mappings
  - workflow context
  - report-ready outputs

Use realistic mock data and local frontend state only. Do not build a real backend or external API integration yet.
